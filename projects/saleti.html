Saleti – Complete Islamic Companion App

Overview

Saleti is a Flutter app for Android with native Kotlin components, built to keep a Muslim's day organized: configurable prayer times with a reliable Azan, a Hijri calendar, a Qibla finder, a full 604-page Mushaf reader, Khatm and Surah-goal tracking, and a personal Du’a journal. It works offline and is designed so the Azan still sounds when the phone is locked, idle, or restarted.

🌙 Prayer Times & Azan

Calculates prayer times from your GPS location, counts down to the next prayer, and delivers reminders and the Azan.

User Perspective

  • Choose the calculation method (Muslim World League, Egyptian, Karachi, Umm al-Qura, Dubai, Singapore, Tehran, ISNA), madhab, and high-latitude rule
  • Fine-tune any prayer by a few minutes to match your local mosque
  • Silent reminders before each prayer, or the full Azan, with per-prayer volume and on/off
  • Live countdown and progress ring for the next prayer, with Jumu’ah labeling on Fridays
  • A readiness banner flags missing permissions (notifications, exact alarms, battery) with one-tap fixes and a Test Azan button
  • Jumu’ah reminder, plus Eid detection with an estimated Eid prayer time

Technical Perspective

  • Azan alarms are armed natively with setExactAndAllowWhileIdle seven days ahead, so a missed daily job can’t cause a missed Fajr
  • Daily rescheduler and post-reboot catch-up keep alarms in sync; date math is DST-safe
  • Kotlin foreground service plays the Azan on the alarm stream with audio focus, and pauses/resumes around phone calls
  • A local Flutter plugin registers the MethodChannel on every engine, including the headless background one
  • Separate notification channels for reminders, Jumu’ah, and Eid

📅 Hijri Calendar

A full Islamic calendar with the major Muslim occasions marked.

User Perspective

  • Navigate months and tap dates for holiday details; Ramadan, Eid, Ashura, and other key events are highlighted
  • Shift Hijri dates by up to two days to match your local moon-sighting announcement

Technical Perspective

  • Hijri dates are calculated on-device (Umm al-Qura based), so the calendar works offline
  • One shared date-adjustment helper keeps the calendar, header date, and Eid detection consistent
  • Custom day builders highlight special dates with colors and icons

🧭 Qibla Finder

Shows the direction to Mecca using your device compass and GPS, with a calibration hint and a clear message on devices without a compass sensor.

Technical Perspective

  • Calculates the Qibla bearing from GPS coordinates and reads the compass sensor stream
  • Heading is tracked in continuous turns so the needle always rotates the short way
  • Only the needle repaints on sensor updates, and the sensors stop when the tab is hidden or the app is backgrounded

📖 Mushaf & Qur’an Reading

Browse all 114 Surahs, read the 604-page Mushaf, bookmark pages, set Surah goals, and track your Khatm.

User Perspective

  • Searchable Surah list with ayah and page counts, jumping straight to the right page
  • Mushaf reader with justified Amiri text, light and dark frames, a distraction-free lecture mode, and the screen kept awake
  • Khatm plan for the year with pages-per-day target, ahead/on-track/behind status, and past-year history
  • Surah goals with target counts, deadlines, and progress
  • Bookmarks that remember the Surah and date

Technical Perspective

  • Mushaf data is parsed on a background isolate and loaded once; justified line layouts are cached per page, width, and font size
  • Khatm and goals persist in Hive with typed adapters and default values for forward compatibility
  • Reading progress is checkpointed every few pages and when the app is paused, so a process kill can’t lose a session
  • PageView.builder navigation through the Mushaf

📝 Du’a Journal

Save favorite Du’as in Arabic or transliteration and read them anytime, in a list or a swipeable gallery, with an adjustable font size for each Du’a.

Technical Perspective

  • Each note stores its own text and font size, so editing or deleting never misaligns sizes
  • Backup and restore through clipboard export/import; imports are additive and never overwrite existing notes
  • Animated hold-to-delete prevents accidental removal
  • Auto-size text and a wakelock keep Du’as readable and the screen on in gallery mode

💡 Technical & UI Highlights

  • Elegant UI with gradients, cards, and a light, dark, or system theme
  • Flutter UI with native Android (Kotlin) services for Azan playback and alarms
  • Offline-first: prayer times, Hijri dates, the Mushaf, and all personal data work without a connection
  • Resilient startup: each init step is isolated, corrupted storage resets itself, and a fallback screen replaces a blank launch
  • Battery-conscious: tabs build on first visit, and timers and sensors pause when not visible
  • Settings and Credits screens, with Amiri (OFL) and open-source license attribution
  • Right-to-left support and Arabic fonts (Amiri)
